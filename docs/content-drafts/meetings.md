# IVC meetings

IVC meetings bring together people who create, maintain, interpret, and use
vaccine vocabularies. They are a place to share current work, examine difficult
coding questions, discuss NUVA and alignment work, and learn from peers.

You do not need to represent an organization or jurisdiction to participate.
Bring your own experience, questions, and interest in making vaccine
information easier to understand.

## Attend or review a meeting

InteropHub is the public home for upcoming and historical IVC meetings. Its
meeting pages include native agendas and, when available, presentation files.

[View the IVC meeting series in InteropHub](https://informatics.immregistries.org/hub/es/meeting-series?seriesId=1)

## Follow topics that interest you

IVC organizes technical subjects through the Building Bridges topic space in
InteropHub. Explore the topics and follow those that matter to you. Following a
topic helps ensure that you are notified when it is discussed in a future IVC
meeting. It also gives IVC a useful signal about which subjects are of greatest
interest.

[Explore Building Bridges topics](https://informatics.immregistries.org/hub/es/topics?space=building-bridges)

Following a topic does not guarantee that it will be scheduled. If an important
subject is missing, please tell us so that we can consider it when planning
future meetings.

[Suggest a topic by emailing info@ivci.org](mailto:info@ivci.org)

## Bordeaux 2025

The May 2025 training and International Summit on Vaccine Coding & Standards in
Bordeaux were important milestones for IVC. The event brought together
vocabulary, standards, public-health, and implementation perspectives and
helped shape the collaboration's current direction.

[Read about Bordeaux 2025](bordeaux-2025.md)
