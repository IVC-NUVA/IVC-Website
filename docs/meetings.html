The best way to participate

Bring your questions and experience to an IVC meeting

IVC meetings connect people who create, maintain, interpret, and use vaccine vocabularies.

View the IVC meeting series

What happens in a meeting?

Meetings are a place to share current work, examine difficult coding questions, discuss NUVA and alignment work, and learn from peers.

You do not need to represent an organization or jurisdiction. Bring your own experience, questions, and interest in making vaccine information easier to understand.

Upcoming and past meetings

InteropHub is the public home for the IVC meeting series. Meeting pages include native agendas and, when available, presentation files.
