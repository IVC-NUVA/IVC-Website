## Historical-content warning

These presentations are historical records, not current policy or evergreen
website guidance. March and April include now-obsolete formal point-of-contact
or liaison concepts and Bordeaux promotion. July includes dated project plans
and claims that require separate verification before reuse. InteropHub should
present them in their meeting context without silently revising their content.

## Exclusions

Do not migrate registrations, attendance, contact details, invitations, chat,
recordings, transcripts, photographs, promotional images, internal reports,
planning documents, follow-up resources, draft decks, conflicted copies, or
duplicate PPTX/PDF renderings. This follows the existing historical-meeting
rule that only selected presentations are attached.
