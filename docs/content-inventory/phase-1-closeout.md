## Explicit deferrals and later-phase work

The following do not block Phase 1 closure:

- final public wording and page-level approval;
- detailed NUVA ownership, governance, identifiers, endpoints, metrics,
  licensing, implementation resources, and publication workflow;
- exhaustive review of every file in the private 612-file source collection;
- a complete crawl of unlinked DokuWiki media;
- missing or private meeting notes, recordings, transcripts, chat, attendance,
  contacts, and photographs;
- optional Bordeaux photographs and accessible HTML session summaries;
- final Bordeaux public-page verification and production meeting URLs;
- FAQ and glossary placement, which belongs to information architecture;
- comments, subscriptions, syndication, and expanded article governance;
- end-to-end testing of `info@ivci.org`, which remains a pre-launch check; and
- InteropHub production deployment of the locally verified historical archive.

These items must be reconsidered only when their later phase or a concrete page
requires them. Their deferral does not authorize publication of unreviewed
material.

## Phase 2 handoff

Phase 2 should define and rank audiences, needs, and use cases using the Phase 1
evidence. It should preserve these fixed boundaries:

- IVC primarily serves vaccine code-set authors through peer collaboration.
- IVC provides technical perspective and best-effort support, not binding
  policy or operational-system authority.
- The launch site is English-only.
- InteropHub is the canonical public meeting archive; the website links to it.
- The website introduces NUVA narrowly and links outward rather than recreating
  detailed or changing technical documentation.
- Static code-system catalogs, comparative metrics, country profiles, formal
  representative roles, and the legacy volunteer model are outside the initial
  website scope.
- Historical artifacts remain dated records and must not be presented as
  current guidance.
