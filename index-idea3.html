A deliberate scope

Focused on complete, meaningful immunization histories.

Immunization events can remain relevant throughout a person’s life. Our work concentrates on the vocabulary knowledge needed to understand what protection a vaccination represents—even when the record crosses borders or code systems.

IVC does not choose code systems for jurisdictions or replace their stewards. It helps experts understand and connect the systems already in use.

Read the full scope

Our work includes

  • Vaccine code-set management
  • National and universal vocabularies
  • Technical concept mapping
  • Lifetime vaccination records
  • Emergency and clinical-trial vaccinations

Outside our scope

  • Policy decisions
  • Choosing a local code system
  • Inventory or supply-chain systems
  • Pharmacovigilance
