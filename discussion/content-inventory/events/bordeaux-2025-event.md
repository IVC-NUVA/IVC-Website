## What the event was

The summit brought together people working with vaccine vocabularies,
terminology, immunization systems, standards, and related implementation
projects. The program combined:

- The practical problem IVC was formed to address
- NUVA's origin, valence model, and role in interpreting vaccination histories
- Work to express NUVA through a SNOMED CT extension
- Industry, pharmacovigilance, European, national, and implementation views
- United States and Canadian vaccine-code experience
- NUVA mapping and code-system assessment methods
- Discussion of future collaboration, governance, support, training, and funding

This description is supported by the final agenda, session decks, Mentimeter
facilitation deck, internal meeting report, and DokuWiki summary. It should not
be expanded into claims of formal consensus or organizational commitments
unless the closing discussion is independently approved as such.

## Substantive outcomes supported by the record

The record supports these cautious conclusions:

- Participants saw recurring difficulty interpreting and exchanging vaccine
  records across code systems and jurisdictions.
- NUVA and its valence model were presented as a practical way to preserve and
  compare the functional meaning of recorded vaccines.
- National, standards, industry, and project perspectives demonstrated that
  vaccine identifiers serve different purposes and cannot be treated as a
  single interchangeable list.
- Mapping, terminology maintenance, and code-system assessment require domain
  knowledge, documented methods, and sustained coordination.
- The closing discussion explored IVC as a collaborative bridge among existing
  organizations rather than automatically creating another large institution.
- Participants discussed governance, funding, community support, training,
  future meetings, and NUVA support, but the reviewed sources do not establish
  these as final decisions.
- The group identified “generic vaccine” as potentially misleading
  pharmaceutical terminology and agreed to prefer “abstract vaccine” for the
  intended concept.

### Corrections and cautions

- The page uses the former organizational name. Preserve that name only when
  describing the historical event, then connect it to the current IVC name.
- It should not imply that the planned WHO or Latin America sessions occurred;
  neither is evidenced in delivered materials or the current public summary.
- The page describes Shannon Coleman's session as “North American Experiences,”
  while Canada had a separate session. “United States experience” is clearer.
- Statements using words such as “only,” “formally integrated,” “consensus,” or
  forward-looking project status should be reviewed by the relevant owner.
- Counts, implementation figures, country lists, project timelines, metric
  percentages, and SNOMED/NUVA status are dated to May 2025.
- The public summary sometimes converts presentation claims into site voice.
  Attribute material to the speaker or presentation when it has not been
  independently verified.
