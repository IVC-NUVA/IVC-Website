## Cross-source findings

1. **Rebranding:** New public content should use Immunization Vocabularies
   Collaboration. Preserve the former name only when accurately labeling a past
   event, file, quotation, or source, with context where needed.
2. **Stewardship ambiguity:** Sources describe NUVA as developed or maintained
   by Syadem, an IVC technical focus, managed by IVCI, or connected to SNOMED.
   The site needs one approved, dated account of responsibilities.
3. **Claims versus history:** Label aspirations, proposals, and meeting outcomes
   by date instead of presenting them as current commitments.
4. **Meeting duplication:** Merge agenda, deck, and notes navigation around a
   single dated event without discarding original artifacts.
5. **Privacy and rights:** Review names, photos, recordings, decks, logos, and
   third-party content before migration.
6. **Accessibility:** Review PDFs and PPTX files and provide useful HTML
   summaries for important information.
7. **Dynamic NUVA data:** Link to authoritative live resources rather than
   duplicating release numbers, counts, mappings, or service status.
8. **Local source collection:** The 612-file working collection has now been
   located and a targeted foundational review is recorded in
   [local-source-review.md](local-source-review.md). Its proposals, Q&A, issue
   brief, orientation template, website plan, and Bordeaux reports materially
   strengthen this inventory, while its private/internal material requires
   strict publication controls.
