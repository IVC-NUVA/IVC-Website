# Bordeaux 2025

In May 2025, the collaboration then known as the International Vaccine Codes
Initiative held two related events in Bordeaux, France:

- an IVC vaccine-code training on 8 May; and
- the International Summit on Vaccine Coding & Standards on 9 May.

The historical IVCI name is retained here because it was the organization's
name at the time.

## Why Bordeaux mattered

The events brought together people working with vaccine vocabularies,
terminology, immunization systems, standards, and implementation projects. The
training introduced vaccine coding and NUVA. The summit examined the practical
problem IVC was formed to address and brought together perspectives on NUVA,
valences, alignments, standards, national experience, industry, and
implementation.

The discussions helped clarify the need for sustained technical collaboration
and shared knowledge. Participants explored IVC as a bridge among existing
organizations and standards efforts—not as a replacement for them. Discussions
about governance, funding, community support, and future work were exploratory
and should not be read as formal commitments or consensus decisions.

## Training — 8 May 2025

The training provided an introduction to vaccine code systems and NUVA ahead
of the summit.

## International Summit — 9 May 2025

Delivered sessions covered:

- the importance of standardized vaccine coding;
- NUVA and its valence model;
- the NUVA extension to SNOMED CT;
- industry and medicinal-product perspectives;
- European, Luxembourg, United States, and Canadian experience;
- the EUVABECO Electronic Vaccination Card project;
- alignment across code systems and related measures; and
- future collaboration and support.

Only sessions supported by the historical record are represented in the
published agenda. Planned WHO and PAHO sessions for which no delivery evidence
was found are not presented as delivered.
