# Contact and participate

The best way to take part in IVC is to attend a meeting. Meetings let you hear
what others are working on, bring a difficult vocabulary question, and share
experience with peers.

[Find an IVC meeting](meetings.md)

## Follow technical topics

Explore the Building Bridges topic space and follow subjects that interest you.
InteropHub can notify you when followed topics are discussed in future IVC
meetings. Topic interest also helps IVC understand what the community would
find most useful.

[Explore and follow Building Bridges topics](https://informatics.immregistries.org/hub/es/topics?space=building-bridges)

## Suggest a topic or ask a question

If a topic is missing, tell us what you would like IVC to consider for a future
meeting. You may also send a vaccine-vocabulary question or point out a needed
correction.

[Email info@ivci.org](mailto:info@ivci.org)

IVC offers best-effort peer support. We cannot guarantee an answer or response
time, and we do not provide operational support for individual vaccination
records. Do not send protected health information or other sensitive personal
information.
