# Making vaccine information easier to understand

Vaccine vocabularies are essential infrastructure. They allow immunization
systems to record which vaccines were given and preserve that meaning as
records move between systems, organizations, and countries.

The specialized knowledge needed to create and maintain those vocabularies is
distributed and often difficult to find. The Immunization Vocabularies
Collaboration (IVC) brings vaccine code-set authors and other terminology
experts together to share practical knowledge, examine difficult questions,
and develop useful technical resources.

[Attend an IVC meeting](meetings.md)

## A shared technical foundation

NUVA—the Unified Nomenclature of Vaccines—is at the center of IVC's technical
work. It is an open, shared ontology for representing administered vaccines
and their valences consistently across existing vaccine code systems and over
time.

NUVA does not replace national, regulatory, product, billing, or clinical code
systems. It provides a shared model that can help people interpret and relate
those systems while their owners remain responsible for their content.
