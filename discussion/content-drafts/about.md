# About the Immunization Vocabularies Collaboration

The Immunization Vocabularies Collaboration is a peer collaboration for the
people who create, maintain, and interpret vaccine code sets.

Vaccine codes are used for different purposes in different systems and
jurisdictions. They also change over time, while a person's vaccination
history may need to remain understandable for a lifetime. The knowledge needed
to manage those differences is specialized, distributed, and not always easy
to find.

IVC gives code-set authors and other terminology experts a place to share that
knowledge and develop practical resources together.

## What we do

IVC currently:

- holds monthly peer meetings;
- develops processes for documenting vaccine code systems;
- supports tools and working methods for aligning code systems with NUVA;
- develops useful measures that support and explain alignment work;
- publishes technical resources and shared knowledge; and
- provides a place to bring technical questions and learn from peers.

Participation is individual. People contribute their own experience; they do
not automatically represent or commit their employers, programs, countries,
or jurisdictions.

## What we do not do

IVC does not set vaccination policy, choose code systems for organizations or
jurisdictions, or replace the bodies that own vocabularies and standards. It
does not manage vaccine supply, medication inventory, program finances,
pharmacovigilance, protected health information, or operational systems.

IVC may offer technical perspectives and best-effort peer support, but it does
not issue binding decisions or guarantee an answer to every question.

## NUVA and IVC

NUVA is the central technical work product around which IVC organizes much of
its current work. IVC provides a collaboration space and helps develop tools,
processes, and extension layers, but it does not currently authorize changes
to the NUVA core. Work toward broader community support is continuing through
NUVA's existing stewardship process.

[Learn about NUVA](nuva.md)

## From IVCI to IVC

The collaboration was formerly called the International Vaccine Codes
Initiative (IVCI). The word “International” was sometimes understood to mean
that the work applied only to cross-border exchange, even though vaccine-code
challenges also occur within countries and organizations.

The name Immunization Vocabularies Collaboration better reflects the work: a
collaboration that supports many vaccine vocabularies without replacing their
owners. Historical presentations and records retain the former name where it
was accurate at the time.
